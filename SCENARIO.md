# Public-source workflow exercise

Reviewed 2026-10-18. This is an independent desktop research exercise, not a customer pilot, a commissioned Field Notes campaign or an actual booking.

## Task

Consider podcast sponsorship for a stationery launch. Field Notes is the cultural brand anchor. A US market and a $1,000 per-partner budget are explicitly invented planning assumptions. Compare The Pen Addict and Accidental Tech Podcast using live Qloo identities plus public commercial descriptions.

## What the public sources establish

| Candidate | Source | What is supported | What remains unknown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Pen Addict | https://www.penaddict.com/sponsor | A listed $300/week website package: sponsor posts and sidebar exposure. | Podcast sponsorship rate, current availability, geographic delivery commitment. The site package is not evidence of a podcast offer at that price. |
| Accidental Tech Podcast | https://atp.fm/sponsor | A podcast sponsorship description includes a host-read segment up to two minutes and an episode-post link. | Rate, availability and a guaranteed US delivery package. An editorial description of the audience is not a geographic guarantee. |

Sources were read publicly without signing up, contacting a publisher, downloading private media kits or purchasing data. Publication recency of a rate is not guaranteed merely because the page is accessible.

## Product consequence

One numeric fee is inadequate. A $20 CPM, $300 weekly rate and $3,000 whole-package quote cannot all be compared directly with a package budget. The product now requests a quote basis and placement channel. Per-episode and weekly rates require the intended unit count. CPM and incomplete pricing stay non-comparable until a usable quote is supplied. Website/newsletter offers cannot stand in for podcast/artist placements.

The public case's correct outcome is not a forced winner: The Pen Addict's supplied website offer does not fit the requested podcast placement; ATP needs more information. Neither outcome says the cultural partner can never be suitable.

## Coverage probe

Convenience sample, exact normalized-name matching among up to five returned choices:

| Type | Exact single match | No exact single match |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand | Topo Designs, Field Notes, Peak Design, Patagonia | Fellow, Owala, Passenger, Tom Bihn |
| Podcast | The Pen Addict, Accidental Tech Podcast, Outside Podcast | The Adventure Podcast |

This is **not a market coverage estimate**. Aliases, additional query variants and manual entity inspection can change outcomes. A non-exact result is not proof that an entity is absent. No automatic proxy brands or invented IDs were substituted. The MVP still requires an identifiable brand anchor; it should not promise universal small-brand support.

## Interaction improvement

For three unresolved identities with prefilled names, the old UI required three modal openings, three Search submissions and three result selections. The batch workflow uses one opening, three explicit selections and one apply action. That is a UI action-count comparison (9 versus 5), not a measured reduction in human task time. Ambiguous identities still require deliberate choice.

Paste import also reduces repetitive field setup, while unknown fees, quantities, markets and placement channels remain unknown. Invalid or duplicate input rejects the import without partially mutating the list.

## Validation scope

Chrome exercised the public case using real Qloo search/scoring, explicit entity confirmation and exported provenance. All metadata is attributable; cultural evidence does not establish ad performance or a buying opportunity. The exercise found practical workflow gaps and tested their fixes. It does not establish willingness to pay or superiority over a spreadsheet, SparkToro, Modash or FanFit.
